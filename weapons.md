## 🗡️ Special Swords

<div class="item-card">
  <h3 class="item-title">Sword of Beheading</h3>
  <div class="item-description">
    <p>An Iron Sword enchanted with Beheading II, which has a chance to drop a Player's or Mob's head upon killing them.</p>
    <div class="special-note">
      <strong>Special Ability:</strong> Chance to drop heads when killing entities
    </div>
    <div class="ingredient-list">
      <h4>Head Drop Chances:</h4>
      <ul>
        <li><strong>Player:</strong> 70% chance</li>
        <li><strong>Creeper:</strong> 40% chance</li>
        <li><strong>Skeleton:</strong> 40% chance</li>
        <li><strong>Zombie:</strong> 40% chance</li>
        <li><strong>Wither Skeleton:</strong> 25% chance</li>
      </ul>
    </div>
    <div class="usage-instructions">
      <strong>Enhancement:</strong> Works even better with the <a href="{{ '/addons/' | relative_url }}">ExtraHeads addon</a> for additional mob heads
    </div>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="item-card">
  <h3 class="item-title">Blade of Vampires</h3>
  <div class="item-description">
    <p>A Gold Sword enchanted with Fire Aspect II, Sharpness II, and Unbreaking IV.</p>
    <div class="special-note">
      <strong>Life Steal:</strong> 45% chance to recover 2 Hearts of health each time you attack
    </div>
    <div class="usage-instructions">
      <strong>Perfect for:</strong> Extended combat situations where health regeneration is crucial
    </div>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="item-card">
  <h3 class="item-title">Soulbound Sword</h3>
  <div class="item-description">
    <p>A special sword that stays with you even after death.</p>
    <div class="special-note">
      <strong>Soulbound:</strong> Will not be dropped upon death - stays in your inventory
    </div>
    <div class="usage-instructions">
      <strong>Advantage:</strong> Never lose this weapon, making it perfect for dangerous adventures
    </div>
  </div>
</div>

  <div class="item-description">

    <div class="special-note">

<div class="tips-section">

  </div>
  
  <div class="tip-card">
    <h4>⚡ Synergy</h4>
    <p>Combine weapons with <a href="{{ '/talismans/' | relative_url }}">Talismans</a> and <a href="{{ '/armor/' | relative_url }}">Enhanced Armor</a> for maximum combat effectiveness.</p>
  </div>
  
  <div class="tip-card">
    <h4>🎯 Strategy</h4>
