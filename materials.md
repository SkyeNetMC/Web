<div class="category-intro">
  <h2>💎 Resources</h2>
  <p>The Resources category contains many natural and man-made resources which are used extensively in Slimefun recipes. These materials form the foundation of all advanced crafting and technology in Slimefun.</p>
  
  <div class="important-info">
    <h3>🔹 Essential Materials</h3>
    <p>Resources are the building blocks of Slimefun technology. From basic dusts to advanced radioactive materials, each resource plays a crucial role in the progression system.</p>

## ⚗️ Dusts

<div class="item-card">
  <h3 class="item-title">Metal Dusts</h3>
  <div class="item-description">
    <p>Fine powder forms of various metals obtained through ore processing.</p>
    <div class="special-note">
      <strong>Essential Processing:</strong> Dusts are created from Sifted Ore using the Ore Washer
    </div>
    <div class="ingredient-list">
      <h4>Common Metal Dusts:</h4>
      <ul>
        <li><strong>Copper Dust:</strong> Essential for early electrical components</li>
        <li><strong>Tin Dust:</strong> Used in bronze production and electronics</li>
        <li><strong>Zinc Dust:</strong> Required for brass and galvanization</li>
        <li><strong>Iron Dust:</strong> Alternative iron source</li>
        <li><strong>Gold Dust:</strong> Precious metal processing</li>
        <li><strong>Silver Dust:</strong> Used in advanced alloys</li>
        <li><strong>Lead Dust:</strong> Heavy metal applications</li>
      </ul>
    </div>
    <div class="usage-instructions">
      <strong>Processing:</strong> Smelt dusts in a <a href="{{ '/machines/' | relative_url }}">Smeltery</a> to create ingots
    </div>
    <div class="automation-info">
      <strong>Automation:</strong> Use <a href="{{ '/electric/' | relative_url }}">Electric Dust Washer</a> for automated dust production
    </div>
  </div>
</div>

## 🔩 Ingots & Alloys

<div class="item-card">
  <h3 class="item-title">Enhanced Ingots</h3>
  <div class="item-description">
    <p>Refined metal ingots and advanced alloys created from dusts and other materials.</p>
    <div class="special-note">
      <strong>Advanced Materials:</strong> Many ingots have superior properties to vanilla metals
    </div>
    <div class="ingredient-list">
      <h4>Key Ingots & Alloys:</h4>
      <ul>
        <li><strong>Copper Ingot:</strong> Basic electrical conductor</li>
        <li><strong>Tin Ingot:</strong> Component in bronze and solder</li>
        <li><strong>Bronze Ingot:</strong> Durable copper-tin alloy</li>
        <li><strong>Brass Ingot:</strong> Copper-zinc alloy for machinery</li>
        <li><strong>Steel Ingot:</strong> Enhanced iron with superior strength</li>
        <li><strong>Damascus Steel:</strong> Premium steel alloy</li>
        <li><strong>Aluminum Ingot:</strong> Lightweight metal for advanced tech</li>
        <li><strong>Duralumin:</strong> Aluminum alloy with enhanced properties</li>
      </ul>
    </div>
    <div class="usage-instructions">
      <strong>Applications:</strong> Used in tools, weapons, armor, and machine construction
    </div>
  </div>
</div>

## 🛠️ Resource Processing Guide

<div class="processing-section">
  <h2>⚙️ Processing Chain</h2>
  <div class="process-flow">
    <div class="process-step">
      <h4>1. 🥄 Gold Pan</h4>
      <p>Use Gold Pan on gravel to get Sifted Ore</p>
    </div>
    <div class="arrow">⬇️</div>
    <div class="process-step">
      <h4>2. 🚿 Ore Washer</h4>
      <p>Process Sifted Ore to extract metal dusts</p>
    </div>
    <div class="arrow">⬇️</div>
    <div class="process-step">
      <h4>3. 🔥 Smeltery</h4>
      <p>Smelt dusts into ingots and alloys</p>
    </div>
    <div class="arrow">⬇️</div>
    <div class="process-step">
      <h4>4. ⚒️ Manufacturing</h4>
      <p>Use ingots to craft tools, weapons, and machines</p>
    </div>
  </div>
</div>
