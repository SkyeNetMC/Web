<div class="category-intro">
  <h2>⚡ Energy & Electricity</h2>
  <p>The Energy and Electricity category adds various machines which generate, consume or transfer energy (measured in <strong>Joules</strong> or <strong>J</strong>). It also includes <a href="{{ '/androids/' | relative_url }}">Programmable Androids</a>, which can be programmed to execute a series of tasks.</p>
  
  <div class="important-info">
    <h3>🔌 Energy Network Requirements</h3>
    <p>Besides a way to generate electricity for your machines, an energy network requires an <a href="{{ '/energy-regulator/' | relative_url }}">Energy Regulator</a>: <strong>without it, power transfer cannot happen</strong>.</p>
    
    <p>You may also want to use <a href="{{ '/energy-capacitors/' | relative_url }}">Energy Capacitors</a>, as they can store excess energy and extend the range of your network. The <a href="{{ '/energy-connector/' | relative_url }}">Energy Connector</a> can also extend your network, but it does not store power.</p>
    
    <div class="tip-box">
      <p><strong>💡 Pro Tip:</strong> Electric machines are best used with a <a href="{{ '/cargo/' | relative_url }}">Cargo Management System</a> for automatic item transportation.</p>
    </div>
  </div>
</div>

## ⚡ Energy Management

<div class="item-card">
  <h3 class="item-title">Energy Regulator</h3>
  <div class="item-description">
    <p>The core component of any electrical network. <strong>Required for power transfer to work.</strong></p>
    <div class="special-note">
      <strong>Critical:</strong> Without an Energy Regulator, no power can be transferred between machines!
    </div>
    <div class="usage-instructions">
      <strong>Function:</strong> Controls and regulates power flow throughout your electrical network
    </div>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="item-card">
  <h3 class="item-title">Energy Connector</h3>
  <div class="item-description">
    <p>Extends the range of your energy network without storing power.</p>
    <div class="usage-instructions">
      <strong>Use Case:</strong> Connect distant machines to your main power grid
    </div>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="item-card">
  <h3 class="item-title">Energy Capacitors</h3>
  <div class="item-description">
    <p>Store excess energy and extend network range. Available in multiple tiers with increasing storage capacity.</p>
    <div class="ingredient-list">
      <h4>Capacitor Tiers:</h4>
      <ul>
        <li><strong>Small Capacitor:</strong> Basic energy storage</li>
        <li><strong>Medium Capacitor:</strong> Improved storage capacity</li>
        <li><strong>Big Capacitor:</strong> Large energy storage</li>
        <li><strong>Large Capacitor:</strong> Massive energy storage</li>
        <li><strong>Carbonado Edged Capacitor:</strong> Ultimate energy storage</li>
      </ul>
    </div>
    <div class="special-note">
      <strong>Important:</strong> Some machines have a buffer, meaning they can store energy. Unlike capacitors, if the energy isn't used before reaching maximum capacity, it will be reset entirely.
    </div>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="item-card">
  <h3 class="item-title">Multimeter</h3>
  <div class="item-description">
    <p>Essential tool for monitoring your electrical network.</p>
    <div class="usage-instructions">
      <strong>Function:</strong> Displays energy information when right-clicking on electrical components
    </div>
  </div>
</div>
