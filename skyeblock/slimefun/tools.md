## 🥄 Resource Gathering Tools

<div class="item-card">
  <h3 class="item-title">Gold Pan</h3>
  <div class="item-description">
    <p>A very important tool that's essential for early game progression. Right-click gravel to sift valuable materials.</p>
    <div class="special-note">
      <strong>Essential Tool:</strong> Required for obtaining Sifted Ore, which is crucial for dust and ingot production
    </div>
    <div class="ingredient-list">
      <h4>Drop Chances:</h4>
      <ul>
        <li><strong>Flint:</strong> 40% chance</li>
        <li><strong>Sifted Ore:</strong> 35% chance</li>
        <li><strong>Clay:</strong> 20% chance</li>
        <li><strong>Iron Nugget:</strong> 5% chance</li>
      </ul>
    </div>
    <div class="usage-instructions">
      <strong>How to use:</strong> Right-click gravel blocks while holding the Gold Pan in your main hand
    </div>
    <div class="automation-info">
      <strong>Automation:</strong> Can be automated with <a href="{{ '/machines/' | relative_url }}">Automated Panning Machine</a> or <a href="{{ '/electric/' | relative_url }}">Electric Gold Pan</a>
    </div>

</div>
