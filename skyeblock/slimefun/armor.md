## 💡 Armor Usage Tips

<div class="tips-section">
  <div class="tip-card">
    <h4>🔧 Set Bonuses</h4>
    <p>Some armor sets like the Hazmat Suit require wearing the complete set to get full benefits. Always check set requirements.</p>
  </div>
  
  <div class="tip-card">
    <h4>⚡ Enchantment Stacking</h4>
    <p>Slimefun armor comes pre-enchanted with high-level enchantments. These often exceed vanilla limits for superior protection.</p>
  </div>
  
  <div class="tip-card">
    <h4>🎯 Situational Use</h4>
    <p>Different armor sets excel in different situations. Keep multiple sets for various environments and challenges.</p>
  </div>
  
  <div class="tip-card">
