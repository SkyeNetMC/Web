<div class="guide-intro">
  <h2>🚀 Getting Started with Slimefun</h2>
  <p>If you have just started using Slimefun it may seem overwhelming or very complicated. You may not know where to start. If that sounds familiar then you have come to the right place!</p>
  <p><strong>This guide assumes that you have already installed Slimefun.</strong> If not, start by reading <a href="{{ '/installation/' | relative_url }}">How to install Slimefun</a>.</p>
</div>

<div class="step-container">
  <div class="step">
    <div class="step-number">1</div>
    <div class="step-content">
      <h3>📖 Slimefun Guide</h3>
      <p>The very first Item you will need on your journey is the <strong>Slimefun Guide</strong>.</p>
      
      <div class="highlight-box">
        <p><strong>How to get it:</strong> Do <code>/sf guide</code> to obtain it and Right-Click the Book to open it.</p>
      </div>
      
      <p>You will now be presented with all Categories Slimefun can offer. In each Category you will find Items that Slimefun (or Addons) have added. You can click on any Item to see its Recipe.</p>
      
      <div class="tip-box">
        <p><strong>💡 Tip:</strong> Take a moment to browse through the guide to familiarize yourself with all the available categories and items.</p>
      </div>
    </div>
  </div>

  <div class="step">
    <div class="step-number">2</div>
    <div class="step-content">
      <h3>🔬 Researching</h3>
      <p>You will notice that a lot of Slimefun Items could be locked (depending on your Server Setup). This means that you will either need a certain permission to use the item or that you have to research the Item first.</p>
      
      <div class="highlight-box">
        <p><strong>How to research:</strong> Simply click on an Item in your Guide that says <span class="locked-item">LOCKED</span>.</p>
      </div>
      
      <div class="warning-box">
        <p><strong>⚠️ Important:</strong> Researching Items will cost you XP levels, so make sure to stock up on Experience before you start!</p>
      </div>
    </div>
  </div>

  <div class="step">
    <div class="step-number">3</div>
    <div class="step-content">
      <h3>🏗️ Multiblocks</h3>
      <p>The very first and most basic Machines are found in the Category <strong>"Basic Machines"</strong>, most of these are actually Multiblocks.</p>
      
      <div class="info-box">
        <p><strong>What are Multiblocks?</strong> Multiblocks are machines that are built in the world using multiple blocks arranged in a specific pattern.</p>
      </div>
      
      <div class="highlight-box">
        <p><strong>How to identify:</strong> You can see whether it is a multiblock by hovering over the Item to the left of the recipe.</p>
        <p><strong>How to build:</strong> The 3rd row of the recipe shows your bottom blocks, then build above them according to the recipe pattern.</p>
      </div>
    </div>
  </div>

  <div class="step">
    <div class="step-number">4</div>
    <div class="step-content">
      <h3>⚒️ Enhanced Crafting Table</h3>
      <p>This is the <strong>first machine</strong> you will need to build. It's essential for crafting most Slimefun items.</p>
      
      <div class="recipe-box">
        <h4>🔧 How to build:</h4>
        <p>Place a <strong>Crafting Table</strong> on top of a <strong>Dispenser</strong>.</p>
      </div>
      
      <div class="highlight-box">
        <p><strong>How to use:</strong> Most Slimefun Recipes are crafted by putting the recipe into the Dispenser, then right-clicking the Crafting Table.</p>
      </div>
      
      <div class="tip-box">
        <p><strong>💡 Learn more:</strong> You can read more about this Machine <a href="{{ '/enhanced-crafting-table/' | relative_url }}">here</a>.</p>
      </div>
    </div>
  </div>

  <div class="step">
    <div class="step-number">5</div>
    <div class="step-content">
      <h3>⛏️ Collecting Dust</h3>
      <p>A lot of Recipes may require various Ingots/Alloys. Here's how to get the materials you need:</p>
      
      <div class="process-chain">
        <div class="process-step">
          <h4>Step 1: Gold Pan</h4>
          <p>Craft a <a href="{{ '/gold-pan/' | relative_url }}">Gold Pan</a> in the <a href="{{ '/enhanced-crafting-table/' | relative_url }}">Enhanced Crafting Table</a>.</p>
        </div>
        
        <div class="arrow">⬇️</div>
        
        <div class="process-step">
          <h4>Step 2: Get Sifted Ore</h4>
          <p>Right-Click Gravel with your <a href="{{ '/gold-pan/' | relative_url }}">Gold Pan</a> and you will get <a href="{{ '/sifted-ore/' | relative_url }}">Sifted Ore</a>.</p>
        </div>
        
        <div class="arrow">⬇️</div>
        
        <div class="process-step">
          <h4>Step 3: Ore Washer</h4>
          <p>Process your <a href="{{ '/sifted-ore/' | relative_url }}">Sifted Ore</a> by building an <a href="{{ '/ore-washer/' | relative_url }}">Ore Washer</a>.</p>
        </div>
        
        <div class="arrow">⬇️</div>
        
        <div class="process-step">
          <h4>Step 4: Collect Dusts</h4>
          <p>You will get lots of dusts (e.g. Copper, Tin, Zinc, ...) from that. Hold onto those and proceed to Step 6.</p>
        </div>
      </div>
    </div>
  </div>

  <div class="step">
    <div class="step-number">6</div>
    <div class="step-content">
      <h3>🔥 The Smeltery</h3>
      <p>Now it's time to turn your Dusts into Ingots!</p>
      
      <div class="highlight-box">
        <p><strong>What to do:</strong> Build a <a href="{{ '/smeltery/' | relative_url }}">Smeltery</a> and smelt your dusts into Ingots or Alloys.</p>
      </div>
      
      <div class="success-box">
        <p><strong>✅ Result:</strong> You can then use these Ingots and Alloys in a ton of recipes throughout Slimefun!</p>
      </div>
    </div>
  </div>

  <div class="step">
    <div class="step-number">7</div>
    <div class="step-content">
      <h3>🎉 Enjoy the plugin</h3>
      <p>Now we should have covered everything you need to know in order to start playing with Slimefun.</p>
      
      <div class="celebration-box">
        <p><strong>🎮 Have fun and enjoy your adventure!</strong></p>
        <p>You're now ready to explore all the amazing features Slimefun has to offer. Check out the different categories in the main menu to discover weapons, tools, machines, and much more!</p>
      </div>
    </div>
  </div>
</div>
